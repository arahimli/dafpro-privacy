DAF Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

DAF Pro is a speech practice app that plays your voice back to you through your headphones with a short delay. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: DAF Pro does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

Microphone

DAF Pro uses the microphone only while you have pressed Start, to play your voice back to you with a delay. The audio is processed on your device in real time. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Recordings

If you tap "Record my speech", the recording is saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. Recordings are not uploaded or backed up by the app. You can play or delete them at any time in Settings > Recordings, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Settings

Your delay, volume and pitch settings are stored on your device so the app remembers them. They do not identify you and never leave your device.

No accounts, ads or tracking

DAF Pro has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app does not access your contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

Children

DAF Pro does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
