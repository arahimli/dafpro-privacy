Getting started

  1. Put on headphones. Wired headphones give the shortest delay.
  2. Set the delay to around 50 to 100 ms.
  3. Press Start, allow microphone access, and read aloud or talk.
  4. Adjust the delay until your speech feels slower and steadier.

Common questions

I hear nothing after pressing Start.

Check that microphone access is allowed (iPhone: Settings > DAF Pro > Microphone) and that your headphones are connected and their volume is up.

I hear echo, squealing or noise.

Without headphones, the speaker feeds back into the microphone. Use headphones, lower the volume boost, and keep the phone away from the earcups.

The delay feels longer than the value I set.

Bluetooth headphones add their own delay, often 150 to 250 ms, on top of the delay you set. Use wired headphones for short delays.

My voice sounds thin or high.

Pitch shift is on. Set Pitch shift to Off for your natural voice.

Does it work with the screen off?

Yes. DAF Pro keeps running in the background and with the screen locked.

Where are my recordings?

Open Settings > Recordings. Recordings are stored only on your device.

Privacy

DAF Pro does not collect any data. Read the privacy policy.
